Local extension of the existing user-supplied YWFT Black Slabbath reference font.
Original: black-slabbath.otf (preserved unchanged).
Extended: black-slabbath-latin.otf.

Adds č, ć, Č and Ć by composing the original c/C and original caron/acute
outlines. Base-letter advance widths are retained. No replacement typeface is
used. Rebuild with scripts/extend-golden-font.py and fontTools.
This extension does not change the underlying font's licensing terms.
